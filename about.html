THE PROJECT BEHIND THE CONNECTIONS

Understand the
Invisible Thread.

Hospitals and pharmacies are part of a larger healthcare ecosystem. INVISIBLE THREAD explores how geographic relationships between healthcare facilities can help us investigate potential disruptions and understand possible impacts across Andhra Pradesh.

01 / OUR PURPOSE

Why INVISIBLE THREAD?

A healthcare facility does not operate in isolation. When access to a facility is disrupted, nearby services and communities may also face challenges. This project provides an interactive way to explore mapped healthcare locations, examine proximity-based connections, and compare hypothetical disruption scenarios.

05 / TRANSPARENCY

What the results do — and don't — mean

Candidate links are not verified dependencies.

The current links are generated using geographic proximity. They do not establish confirmed supply routes, patient flows, referrals, shared resources, or operational relationships. Scenario outputs are exploratory and should not be used alone for real-world healthcare decisions.

Data completeness, geographic coverage, assumptions, and the absence of verified operational relationship data may affect the results. Further validation with reliable real-world evidence is needed.
