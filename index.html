See the connections. Understand the ripple effects.

Cities depend on networks we rarely see. When one part is disrupted, the effects can spread across essential services. INVISIBLE THREAD explores these connections to help people understand risk before a disruption becomes a wider problem.

The approach

From a single disruption to a wider picture.

A network-first approach helps reveal which services may be connected, where further investigation is needed, and how a local disruption could create wider operational concerns.
